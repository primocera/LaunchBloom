# SC-V12-06 · Scalvya — Sharpen agency-first conversion without inflating claims

**Run after:** SC-V12-05.
**Primary risk removed:** the hero addresses several audiences at once and can dilute the
strongest willingness-to-pay use case.

## Prompt

Perform a surgical conversion-content pass on Scalvya's public acquisition and first-run
surfaces. Do not redesign the entire landing page and do not add fabricated proof.

Primary ICP: freelance marketers and boutique agencies delivering multi-channel launches for
clients.
Secondary ICP: solo founders and small brands using the same workflow for their own launch.

Content requirements:
1. Keep the canonical promise: "Turn one offer into a launch-ready campaign."
2. Lead the hero supporting copy with the primary ICP and the expensive problem: keeping offer,
   claims, CTA and assets consistent across channels.
3. Keep solo founders visible below the primary explanation or as a concise secondary sentence.
4. Define launch-ready exactly as structured, connected and ready for human review. Never imply
   approved, compliant, published or guaranteed performance.
5. Preserve the complete product journey: Brand Profile -> Campaign Brief -> Create -> Review ->
   Library -> Export.
6. Preserve exactly five creation paths: Website, Email, Social, Ads & Creative, SEO Ideas.
7. Keep Social Calendar as planning only and SEO as ideation requiring real research.
8. Keep free workspace editing separate from the 3-day paid trial triggered when generation
   begins.
9. Tighten repeated wording across landing, pricing, signup, onboarding and paywall. Do not remove
   buyer-critical disclosures.
10. Make the first 60 seconds answer: Is this for me? Why not use a generic chat? What will I
    receive? What remains my responsibility? When will I be charged?
11. Do not invent customer logos, testimonials, usage counts, time-saved claims, urgency or
    integrations.

UX validation:
- Verify CTA destinations, focus order, 320px layout, 200% zoom, reduced motion and no horizontal
  scroll.
- Verify the real example remains clearly labelled as synthetic/example data.
- Add rendered-copy contract tests for the promise, trial, responsibilities and banned claims.

Report every copy change as before -> after with the reason. Keep changes that do not materially
improve clarity out of the diff.
