## Top-up design (final, owner-approved)

**Hard rule from the owner:** build ON TOP of what exists, never rework.
Saved in memory as `build-on-top-never-rework`.

A weighted-credits redesign (Higgsfield-style, renaming `ai_actions` to `credits`)
was built and then **rejected and fully reverted**. Do not bring it back.

- The model is unchanged: 1 generation = 1 AI action. Limits: trial 20 / starter 30 /
  pro 120 / studio 400. All existing copy and fields are unchanged.
- Packs:
  - `actions_40`: $9.99 / €8.70
  - `actions_100`: $24.99 / €21.76
  - Defined in `backend/lib/plan-catalog.js` `TOPUP_PACKS`, with env
    `STRIPE_PRICE_ACTIONS_40` / `STRIPE_PRICE_ACTIONS_100`.
  - Only packs with a configured price are offered (`publicCatalog().topups`).
- Who can buy: starter/pro/studio, monthly AND yearly (`topupPlan()`). The UI offers
  packs only when a price is also configured (`canBuyTopup()`).
- Validity: only until the next monthly reset of the usage window. There is no
  rollover ("zakup sam za tist mesc"). Yearly plans also reset monthly.
- Backend:
  - `POST /api/payments/create-topup-checkout` creates a Stripe Checkout with
    `mode=payment` and metadata `{scalvya:'1', kind:'action_topup', pack, app_user_id}`.
  - In `webhooks.js`, `grantTopupPack()` runs inside `onCheckoutSessionCompleted`
    and also on `checkout.session.async_payment_succeeded`.
    - It upserts into `action_topups` with a unique `stripe_checkout_session_id`.
    - The expiry is computed at grant time with `usage.customerWindowEnd()`.
    - It throws on a DB read error so Stripe retries.
  - A full refund (`charge.refunded`) or a LOST dispute (`charge.dispute.closed`)
    sets the row to `status = 'refunded'`.
  - Packs are **never** written to `payments`: `resolveEntitlement` treats any
    succeeded payment there as lifetime Pro.
  - `planGate`: `poolLimit = plan ai_actions + usage.activeTopupActions(userId)`.
  - The 402 body gets `can_buy_topup`.
  - `usageFor` adds `ai_actions_extra`, but only when it is greater than 0.
- Frontend:
  - `app-src/components/TopupDialog.jsx`.
  - The Account page has a "Buy more AI actions" button and opens the dialog on
    `?topup=1`.
  - `ErrorState` `BUY_TOPUP` links to `/app/account?topup=1`.
  - EmailFlowStudio links to the same place.
  - The Sidebar shows "+N extra from top-ups".
  - `App.jsx` shows a notice for `?checkout=topup_success`.
- Tests:
  - New: `backend/tests/action-topups.test.js` (webhook grant, expiry math,
    idempotency, refund, dispute) and `backend/tests/topup-gate.test.js`.
  - Appended to `payments.test.js`.
- Migration `backend/migrations/041_action_topups.sql`:
  - Owner-applied, recorded as owner-pending in `docs/launch/launch-state.json`.
  - Until it is applied, the gate counts 0 extra actions and grants retry via Stripe.
- Safe to push before setup: with no Stripe price env set, users see nothing new.

## Known minor items, deliberately not fixed (additive-only rule)

- `usage.windowResetsAt` ("resets on" date) can be a few days early for
  subscriptions anchored on the 29th–31st. Pack expiry uses the correct `monthlyWindowEnd`.
- `usage.windowStart` looks the customer up by email, not app_user_id.
- The top-up checkout has no Stripe idempotency key. A double click opens 2
  sessions, but the buyer is charged only once. The subscription checkout behaves the same.
